What JalDrishti Uncovers

What is this Platform Trying to Tell Us?

Beyond mechanical sensor readings, JalDrishti reveals four critical, data-grounded truths about India's canal networks that have remained unseen for decades.

1. The "Clear Water" Illusion

Invisible Contaminants Passing Standard Tests

Rural irrigation water often looks visually clear and passes standard government pH/TDS tests. However, standard testing completely misses sub-millimeter polyethylene mulch shreds (LDPE) and carcinogenic polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) from stubble burning. These microscopic residues pass through traditional intake filters directly into food crops and municipal water intakes.

Over 840 invisible micro-fragments per liter carry toxic plasticizers

2. Precision Source Pinpointing

Replacing Blanket Penalties with Evidence

Traditional pollution enforcement frequently levies blanket fines and blame on entire regional farming communities during harvest burning periods. JalDrishti pairs autonomous downstream canal water optical detections with Sentinel-2 satellite thermal fire maps to mathematically back-calculate stream flow vectors, pinpointing the specific sub-basin cluster responsible while protecting sustainable farmers.

Traces downstream spikes to upstream origin clusters in <15 minutes

3. The Farmland Soil Choke

How Canal Residue Degrades Agricultural Yields

When microplastic mulch fragments enter canal distributaries and flood farm fields, they accumulate in topsoil, creating impermeable micro-barriers. This impairs root aeration by up to 35%, traps pesticide chemical residues in the root zone, and leads to chronic crop yield degradation. Real-time canal monitoring stops plastic accumulation before it destroys soil health.

Protects agricultural topsoil fertility across 12,000+ canal-fed acres

4. Proactive Drinking Water Defense

Automated Intake Valve Shutoff Protocols

Canal networks directly feed raw water to drinking treatment facilities in towns and rural villages. Standard coagulation systems cannot bind nano-plastics or dissolved ash PAHs. JalDrishti provides automated early warning telemetry that alerts water works engineers to close intake sluice gates hours before toxic contamination pulses reach reservoirs.

Automated push alerts trigger intake diversions to prevent reservoir contamination
